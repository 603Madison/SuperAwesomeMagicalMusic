## New

- **Dead-zone cache.** Songs you play are kept on the phone, and the next few in the queue are
  fetched ahead while the connection is good (Wi-Fi, or cellular if you allow it; never on
  satellite or with Data Saver on). A dead zone or a stretch on satellite no longer stops the
  music, and a song cached whole plays with no connection at all. Settings › Dead-zone cache
  sets the space (512 MB to 4 GB), how many songs to fetch ahead, and whether to use cellular;
  the least recently played songs make room first. Downloads are separate and never removed.
- **Listening history** (Playlists › Listening history): everything you've played and heard,
  songs on SiriusXM channels and Pandora stations included, by day. Save any of them to a
  playlist with one tap. The latest also show under **Recently played** on the Search tab.
- **Scrobbling** (Settings › Scrobbling) to ListenBrainz, or a self-hosted server with its API
  (Maloja, Koito…), and to Last.fm with your own API key. Songs heard on SiriusXM and Pandora can
  be included. Listens made offline are kept and sent once you're back online.
- **Start an artist station from any song** (in the song's menu), through SiriusXM.
- **Save Pandora thumbs-ups** to a "Liked on Pandora" playlist (Settings › Playback; off by default).
- **Resume in the car.** The app remembers your queue, so Android Auto's "start music
  automatically", your car's play button, or the system media controls pick up where you left
  off. Turn on Settings › Playback › Resume in the car to also start playing whenever Android
  Auto connects.
- **Get ready for a trip** (Downloads › Trip): downloads your most played songs, what you've
  played lately, and any playlists you pick, in one go.
- **Lidarr downloads.** When Lidarr has downloaded music by an artist it added for you, you get a
  note, and the artist shows as waiting until then in Settings › Lidarr.
