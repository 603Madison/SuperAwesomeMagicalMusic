## A Now Playing page worth showing off (web player)

- **The cover's colours fill the page.** A huge, blurred copy of the album art drifts slowly behind
  everything, and crossfades when the song changes. The cover glows in its own colour.
- **Lyrics that follow the song.** The line being sung is big and bright, and stays a third of the way
  down; the others fade back. Click any line to jump there. Scroll back through them yourself and
  they wait a few seconds, with a "Back to the song" button.
- **Nothing jumps around.** The page itself never scrolls: only the lyrics and the queue do, in
  their own panels, so the cover stays put.
- **Fits every screen.** Wide windows show the cover beside the lyrics or what's up next. Narrower
  windows and phones get tabs (Song, Lyrics, Up next), with the song shown small above its lyrics.
  On phones the Song tab has a scrubber and skip buttons, which the phone's player bar doesn't.
- **Full screen** (the button in the corner) for parties and the TV, with its own controls.
- The lyrics button in the player bar opens straight to the lyrics.

## Live radio

- **The song's own cover.** When SiriusXM doesn't show one, the cover of your own copy is used:
  from your library, else YouTube Music.
- **Favourites.** A heart on the Now Playing page and in the player bar keeps a SiriusXM channel
  or radio station; your favourites are at the top of the Radio page, one tap to play.
- **What played before.** The Played tab lists the channel's last three hours (SiriusXM), or what a
  station played since you tuned in. Any of those songs can be saved to a playlist, played or
  looked up.
- **How far into the song.** For SiriusXM, the song's progress and length, in the player bar and
  on the Now Playing page.
- The channel shows above the song with its logo and number, and **LIVE** shows once in the player
  bar (it was there twice), centred, with a beating dot.

## Fixes

- **The progress bar no longer gets stuck.** Clicking or dragging it could leave it frozen at that
  spot, even into the next song. Now the song moves where you let go (anywhere, even off the bar),
  the bar follows the song otherwise, and each song starts with it in the right place.
- **Playlists made on the web show up on your phone** (SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic app) as soon as you
  open the Playlists tab, and you can pull it down to refresh. Before, they could take up to an hour
  if the app was already open. One playlist that can't be read no longer holds up the others.
