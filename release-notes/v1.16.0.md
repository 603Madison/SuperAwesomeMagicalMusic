## A Now Playing page worth showing off (web player)

- **The cover's colours fill the page.** A huge, blurred copy of the album art drifts slowly behind
  everything, and crossfades when the song changes. The cover glows in its own colour.
- **Lyrics that follow the song.** The line being sung is big, bright and stays a third of the way
  down; the others fade back. Click any line to jump there. Scroll back through them yourself and
  they wait a few seconds, with a "Back to the song" button.
- **Nothing jumps around.** The page itself never scrolls: only the lyrics and the queue do, in
  their own panels, so the cover stays put.
- **Fits every screen.** Wide windows show the cover beside the lyrics or what's up next. Narrower
  windows and phones get tabs (Song, Lyrics, Up next), with the song shown small above its lyrics.
  On phones the Song tab has a scrubber and skip buttons, which the phone's player bar doesn't.
- **Full screen** (the button in the corner) for parties and the TV, with its own controls.
- The lyrics button in the player bar opens straight to the lyrics.

No changes to the Android apps; they move to 1.16.0 to keep one version number for everything.
