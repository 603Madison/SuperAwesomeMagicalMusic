## Wrapped: your year in music

Like Spotify Wrapped, from everything you play through your server: **Wrapped** in the web
player's sidebar (and a banner on History), and a banner on the app's Search tab.

- **A story to tap through**: minutes listened, your song and artist of the year, your top five
  of each, your station of the year, artists you discovered, your music clock, your longest
  streak, your listening personality, and a summary card at the end. Tap the right of the screen
  for the next slide, the left to go back; hold (or press pause) to stop. On a computer the arrow
  keys and space work too.
- **The recap**: top songs (tap to play), artists and albums, minutes by hour of the day and by
  month, where it came from (Jellyfin, YouTube Music, SiriusXM, …), on which devices, your live
  radio stations, and highlights: biggest day, longest streak, the song you had on repeat, your
  first song of the year, discoveries and your personality.
- **Save top songs as a playlist**: "Your Top Songs 2026", up to 50 of them.
- **Any year**: this year so far, or earlier years you listened in.

What counts:

- Songs count as before (half the song, or four minutes, and at least 30 seconds). The same song
  from different services (or "feat." versions) counts as one.
- **Live radio counts now**: songs heard on SiriusXM channels (Xtra channels too) are kept as
  listens with the channel's name, in the app and the web player; time on a channel without a
  song (talk) or on a radio station counts toward its minutes. They show in History too.
- Your server now keeps your last 100,000 listens (it kept 5,000), so earlier listening from this
  year may already be gone; next year's Wrapped will have all of it.

(The Lite app has no server, so no Wrapped.)
