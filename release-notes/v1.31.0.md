## Now Playing: music around you, recognised by itself

Like Pixel phones' Now Playing. Turn it on in the phone app (**Settings › Listen**, or on the
Listen screen) and the phone recognises music playing around you without being asked.

- **What you see:** the song in a quiet notification, on the lock screen too. It's also added to
  **Recently heard** everywhere.
- **How it listens:** every minute or so (30 seconds to 5 minutes, your choice) it listens for
  three seconds. If that sounds like music, it listens for seven more.
- **Privacy:** the phone works out the song's fingerprints itself and sends only those to your
  server. The sound never leaves the phone, and fingerprints can't be turned back into it.
- **Battery:**
  - After recognising a song, it waits until the song should be over.
  - It listens less and less often while it hears music it doesn't know.
  - It doesn't listen while the phone plays music itself, during a call, in battery saver, or
    when the battery is low.
- **After the phone restarts:** tap the notification (or open the app) to carry on. Android
  requires that.
- **What it can recognise:** only songs in your library. AudD needs the sound itself, so it's
  left to **Listen**.

## The Tuner: your local FM and HD Radio

A new, small container for a Raspberry Pi with RTL-SDR USB dongles. Like an HDHomeRun, but for
the radio: it brings local FM and HD Radio stations to your server.

- **Now playing:** stations come with their name and the song and artist from the broadcast
  (RDS and HD Radio). HD Radio stations also show the station's logo and the album art. In the
  web player you get the song's cover and lyrics, and it says whether it's playing from your
  tuner or online.
- **Clearest copy:** a station that's on your tuner and also online is listed once. It plays the
  tuner while reception is clear, and the internet stream when the tuner has static or is busy.
  It switches by itself with a short crossfade.
- **Bandwidth:** the tuner only tunes while someone is listening. The internet copy is only
  fetched while it's the one playing.
- **Setting it up:**
  1. Run `ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalmusic-tuner` (amd64 and arm64) with your dongles
     plugged in. `tuner/README.md` has Docker, Podman and Compose setups.
  2. Scan for stations on its own page (`http://your-pi:5004`).
  3. Add it in **Settings › Sources › FM tuner**.
  - Each dongle tunes one frequency at a time. HD2 and HD3 share their station's dongle.
- **Hardware:** it's been tested with recorded and made-up signals, not yet with dongles on the
  air. How it decides "clear" and "static" may need tuning against your stations.

## Mini: an app for flip phones

**SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic Mini** is for Android flip phones (TCL Flip, Kyocera DuraXV, Sonim,
CAT S22 Flip and the like). Download `SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Mini-v1.31.0.apk`, or get it from
the F-Droid repo.

- **Keypad:**
  - The D-pad moves a big highlight and OK plays.
  - The soft keys open options and go back.
  - The number keys pick from menus.
- **What it has:**
  - search;
  - your playlists;
  - your own radio stations and favourites;
  - recently played;
  - thumbs;
  - a sleep timer;
  - background playback.
- **Data:** it streams at 96 kbps by default.

## Sign in with a code

No more typing a long password on a tiny keypad.

- **On Mini:** it shows a short code (and a QR code). Go to your server's `/link` page on your
  phone or computer, check which device is asking, and tap **Allow**. It works with single
  sign-on too.
- **Phone app:** the sign-in screen has **Sign in with a code from another device**.
- **Web player:** **Settings › Signed in on** has **Sign in a device with a code**.

Update the server first.
