## Qobuz is gone from Settings

Qobuz's API only works with an app ID and secret that Qobuz gives its partners, so Qobuz can no
longer be set up:

- **Lite:** the Qobuz sign-in is gone from Settings, along with the "try first" choice (YouTube
  Music is the one streaming service left).
- **Server:** Qobuz is no longer offered under Add a source. A Qobuz source added before keeps
  working.

Songs saved from Qobuz in your playlists still play, from your library or YouTube Music.

## Importing Qobuz playlists

Qobuz doesn't share playlists in a way apps can read: its website turns apps away, and its web
player only loads songs through that partner API. So a Qobuz link can't be imported directly.
Pasting one now explains what to do instead:

1. Export the playlist as a CSV file with a service such as TuneMyMusic or Soundiiz.
2. Paste the CSV under **Import › From a list**.

Links from Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, Deezer, YouTube and ListenBrainz import as before, with no
accounts needed.
