## Your own radio stations, fixed

Your stations (Your Radio, Rewind and Discovery) could get out of step with what you were
hearing. This release fixes that:

- **What's shown is what you hear.** Your player now tells the server how far into the stream it
  is, and the server says which song that is. The song shown, the album art, the lyrics, the
  thumbs and the DJ all follow what's playing in your ears.
  - Before, they followed the station itself. A player that had fallen behind (each hiccup while
    loading leaves it further back) showed the next song while still playing the last one.
- **Skip skips what you hear.** If your player has fallen behind the station, it tunes in again,
  so you hear the next song straight away.
- **No more songs changing part way through.** If a service stops sending a song early, the
  station now picks it up again where it stopped, instead of jumping to the next song.
- **Faster to tune in.** A song that's slow to start is given up on after 15 seconds and the next
  one plays. Before, the station could wait in silence for a long time.
  - Tuning in also no longer starts partway through a song from a service that can't jump ahead
    quickly.
- **The phone app no longer goes silent after a dropped connection.** It used to wait, silent,
  for as long as you'd been listening before playing again. It now tunes in again straight away.
- **Pausing lets go of the station.** Pausing one of your stations disconnects it (on the phone,
  after 15 seconds), so the station stops fetching songs. Playing again tunes in to what's on
  then. If your player falls more than 25 seconds behind, it catches up by itself.
- **The DJ only talks over the start of a song,** never the middle of one.

**Bandwidth:** a station only fetches music while someone is listening. It goes off 20 seconds
after the last listener leaves (it was 45), and fetches nothing once it's off. While you listen, it
fetches the song playing and starts the next one about 20 seconds before the change.

## Lite is now Standalone

The app that works on its own, without a server, is now called **SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic
Standalone**. It's the same app: it updates in place, and nothing else changes. On the releases
page it's `SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Standalone-v1.30.1.apk`.

Update the server first.
