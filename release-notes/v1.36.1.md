## The desktop app looks like the web player

- **The web player's icons:** the desktop app draws the same icons as the web player in the
  sidebar, the player bar, Now playing, menus and buttons, in place of the system's. They're sized
  as on the web: 20px in the sidebar, 18px in the player bar, bigger to skip and play.
- **Settings in the sidebar**, between Wrapped and Admin, as on the web. It opens Preferences.
- **The player bar's buttons** are round and muted until you point at them, as on the web, and the
  play button is in the theme's colour.
- **Now playing's controls** are filled and bigger, as on the web.
- **Playlists:** covers line up with their names, the cards say how many songs (or who shares it),
  and there's no line under the title.
- **Radio:** your own stations sit three across, the stations are a plain list, and there's no line
  under the title.
