## YouTube Music on the phone: your server first, YouTube as the backup

- **The setting "Get YouTube Music on this phone" is gone.** Songs always come through your
  server first: it's quicker, on mobile data too.
- **The phone asks YouTube directly only as a backup**, for a YouTube Music song when:
  - your server can't be reached, or can't get the song;
  - your server's copy fails to play; or
  - nothing of it has loaded after 20 seconds.

  After 30 minutes the song is tried through your server again.
- **The (i) details say when the backup was used** ("This phone, straight from YouTube (your
  server couldn't play it)").

(The SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic app only; the server and the Lite app are unchanged.)
