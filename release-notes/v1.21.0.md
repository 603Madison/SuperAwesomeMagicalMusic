## Claude connector (MCP)

- **The server is now an MCP server**, so Claude can look at it to help troubleshoot. In the web
  player, open **Settings › Claude connector › Make a connector link**, and add the link in
  Claude under **Settings › Connectors › Add custom connector**. Claude can then see the server's
  status, its latest warnings (admins), SiriusXM's stream log, stream tests of any song, your
  devices, search and your listening history. It can only look, never change anything, and sees
  only what your account sees. The link is listed with your sign-ins (**Signed in on**), where
  it's removed. See `server/README.md`.

## Web player

- **Thumbs up and down for Pandora station songs on the Now Playing page.** They were only in the
  player bar, which phones don't show in full.
- **Phones: how far into the song, along the top of the player bar**, on every page (the bar has
  no room for the seek bar, so there was none outside the song's page).
- **Phones: the song page's seek bar is easier to hit**: a taller touch area and a bigger knob
  (it was a 5-pixel line).

## SiriusXM Xtra channels

- **SiriusXM's stream log starts with an Xtra timeline**: a short line for each tune and peek
  (what was added, and how much was queued), each track that grew, the song being heard, and when
  the player ran out of queued music waiting for SiriusXM. It shows where a channel like Alt Nation
  Advanced Placement stalls (through the Claude connector, or the stream log link in the web
  player's details).
