## SiriusXM Xtra channels on the web: found and fixed

Alt Nation Advanced Placement (and other Xtra channels) played for a while, then spun forever.
The server's log, read through the Claude connector, showed why: the browser jumped straight to
the **end** of the songs queued for the channel, played their last half minute, and ran dry,
while the server, thinking you were at the start, saw no need to ask SiriusXM for more.

- **The web player now plays every stream through hls.js**, where the browser supports it (every
  current browser, iPhones included). Chrome and Edge have started playing HLS themselves, and
  their built-in player starts a live playlist at its end, ignoring the playlist's own "start
  here". That's fine for ordinary live channels (regular Alt Nation worked), but an Xtra
  channel's playlist holds several songs queued ahead.
- **A channel starts at the first song SiriusXM hands out** when you tune in (it started at the
  newest of the queued songs, skipping the ones before it), and a player reconnecting starts at
  the song being heard. This applies to the apps too.
- With hls.js playing, the server also knows exactly where you are in the stream, so it asks
  SiriusXM for the next songs a minute before the queue runs out.
