## SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic for Android

A new app (installed alongside Lite): **SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic**, for your server. Download
`SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-v1.14.0.apk`, or get it from the F-Droid repo, which now lists both apps.

- **Sign in to your server.** Enter its address, then sign in with Authentik (or whatever single
  sign-on the server uses) in a browser tab, or with a password. The browser hands the app a
  one-time code that only this app can use. Sign the phone out from the server's Settings › devices
  at any time.
- **Everything on the server.** Search your library and every service at once; SiriusXM channels
  with what's on now, Pandora artist stations with thumbs, and local radio; your playlists, the
  same as on the web; lyrics, a sleep timer and volume levelling.
- **Offline first.** Playlists are kept on the phone and work with no connection. Changes you make
  offline (new playlists, songs added or removed, renames) are sent, in order, when you're back
  online. Songs that aren't downloaded are greyed out while offline.
- **Downloads.** Download songs, or turn on *Keep offline* for a playlist to download all of it
  and keep up with songs added later. Downloads wait for Wi-Fi by default, pick up where they
  stopped when the connection drops, and play with no connection at all.
- **Satellite.** Quality is set separately for Wi-Fi, mobile data and satellite (64 kbps by
  default; the server converts down to it). On satellite the player loads ten minutes ahead and
  pictures are kept small. Android's Data Saver is respected too.
- **Android Auto**: your playlists (even offline), downloads, SiriusXM channels and radio, with
  search; and "play … on SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic" by voice.
- History: what you listen to goes to the server (later, if you're offline).
