## The desktop app, closer to the web player

The Linux app now looks and works much more like the web player:

- **Text at the web player's size** (15px, Lexend), so pages read the same on both. It's set in
  points, so the desktop's text scaling still applies.
- **Song lists as the web player's:** each row has its number and cover, the title, then the
  artist and album, sitting on the page rather than in a boxed card. A row lights up under the
  pointer, with a play button over its cover. **The song that's playing** shows in the theme's
  colour with a pause button, in every list on screen. Clicking it pauses it.
- **Every song's ⋯ menu** has Play next, Add to queue, **Add to playlist** (New playlist…, or any
  of yours), Go to artist and Go to album. In a playlist it also has **Move up**, **Move down** and
  **Remove from this playlist**.
- **Playlists:** covers as a **mosaic** of four (on the page, in Playlists and in the sidebar).
  Next to Play and Shuffle are **Add to queue, Rename, Export CSV and Delete**, as on the web.
  Artist and album pages gain **Add to playlist**.
- **The sidebar** marks the playlist that's open (no longer "Search") and the station that's
  playing.
- **The player bar** has **shuffle** (what's next) and **repeat** (off, all, or this song; it
  stays set). The progress bar is the web's width, and there's a line saying how the song is
  playing (like "FLAC (original file)"). There's a **star** for the live station that's playing,
  a **sleep timer** (in 15–90 minutes, or at the end of the song) and a **mute** button. The slider
  knobs show when you point at them, as on the web.
- In a window under about 760 pixels wide, the sidebar folds away and the player bar drops
  shuffle, repeat, the sleep timer and the volume slider, as the web player does on small
  screens.
- Playing a playlist (not just opening it) puts it first among your playlists, as on the web.

Checked by clicking through it in the dark and light styles, wide and narrow:

- Playing from a playlist, the playing row and the pointer highlight.
- Moving a song down.
- Adding a song to a playlist, and making a new playlist from a song's menu.
- Renaming, exporting to CSV and deleting a playlist.
- Repeat.

The phone apps and the web player are unchanged in this release.

### Install

- **Ubuntu 24.04 and later:** `sudo apt install ./SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Desktop-v1.28.0-amd64.deb`
- **Fedora 40 and later:** `sudo dnf install ./SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Desktop-v1.28.0-x86_64.rpm`
- **Phone:** `SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-v1.28.0.apk` and `SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Lite-v1.28.0.apk`
  (only the version number changed).

Server image: `ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalmusic:1.28.0` (unchanged apart from the
version).
