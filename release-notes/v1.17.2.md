## Favourite stations on your phone

- **The Radio tab shows your favourites at the top**: the same SiriusXM channels and radio stations
  you favourited in the web player, one tap to play. Hold one to remove it.
- **A heart on Now Playing** for channels and stations makes one a favourite on the phone, and it
  shows up in the web player too. Favourite channels have a small heart in the SiriusXM list.
- **Android Auto**: Radio › Favourites.

## Export playlists as CSV

- **Every playlist can be saved as a CSV file**: in the web player (Export CSV, beside Rename), the
  SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic app (Export CSV, beside Rename) and the Lite app (the playlist's ⋮ menu).
  On the phone you pick where it goes (Downloads, Drive…).
- The columns are Exportify's (Track Name, Artist Name(s), Album Name, Duration (ms), ISRC), so the
  file opens in a spreadsheet, imports into other services (TuneMyMusic, Soundiiz…), and imports
  back into this app as the same songs.

Server image: `ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalmusic:1.17.2`.
