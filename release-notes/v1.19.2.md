## Quality settings on the phone

- **Android's Data Saver held songs to 128 kbps**, whatever was chosen for Wi-Fi or mobile data
  (it showed as "AAC 128 kbps (transcoded)"). Settings › Quality now has **"With Data Saver on"**,
  like the Lite app: choose Original there and your other choices apply with Data Saver on too.
  When Data Saver is what's holding the quality down, Settings says so.
- **The (i) details say why** a song is at the quality it is: the network it was picked on, or
  that Data Saver held it down.
- **A new quality setting applies from the next song.** Songs fetched ahead kept the old quality
  for up to ten minutes; now that only happens when the network itself flips back and forth.

(The SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic app only; the server and the Lite app are unchanged.)
