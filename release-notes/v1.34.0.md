## Shared playlists

Share a playlist with other people on your server, and make it together.

- **Sharing:** open one of your playlists in the web player or the phone app, choose **Share**
  and pick who. You can take anyone off again.
- **Making it together:**
  - Everyone it's shared with sees it with their own playlists.
  - Everyone adds, removes and reorders its songs.
  - Each song says who added it ("added by Sam").
- **The owner's controls:**
  - Only the person who made it renames it, deletes it or shares it with more people.
  - Everyone else can **Leave** it, and the songs they added stay.
- **Last played:** each person's is their own.
- **In the phone app:** a shared playlist syncs, downloads and takes changes made offline like
  any other.
- **Elsewhere:** the lite web player and the desktop app show who shares a playlist and who added
  each song.

Update the server first, then the phone app.
