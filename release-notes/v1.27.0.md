## Aurora, and themes everywhere

The family's new look is **Aurora**: purple and green, like the northern lights, over cool slate
greys, with a soft glow of the two at the top of every page. It's the default in the web player,
the phone app and the desktop app.

**Themes:** pick another in Settings › Appearance (Preferences › Appearance on the desktop):

- **Aurora**: purple and green (the default)
- **Violet**: the violet and rose from before
- **Ocean**: blue and teal
- **Ember**: burnt orange and rose

Each comes light and dark, and **Style** chooses light, dark, or as the system is (new in the web
player too). On the phone, "Wallpaper colours" still takes your wallpaper's colours instead.

### The desktop app, in the family's look

The Linux app now looks like the web player and the phone app, not like a stock GNOME app:

- **Lexend** throughout, and **Bold text** in Preferences.
- A sidebar like the web player's: the sections as pills, then **Your radio** (your favourite
  stations, one click to play) and **Your playlists** (most recently played first).
- Large page titles over the theme's glow, a rounded search field, the **top result** as a card in
  the theme's gradient, and songs in rounded cards.
- Artist, album and playlist pages with a large cover (round for artists) and Play and Shuffle.
- **Radio** with a Favourites | SiriusXM | Local radio switch; stars now show lit for the channels
  and stations you've already starred, and Favourites shows new stars straight away.
- Playlists as a grid of covers.
- Empty pages with a round icon, as the other apps show them.
- The sign-in as a card over the glow, like the web player's.
- The player bar restyled, and it now fits narrow windows, so below about 640 pixels the sidebar
  folds away behind a back button.

The Lite app is unchanged.

### Install

- **Phone:** `SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-v1.27.0.apk` (and `SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Lite-v1.27.0.apk`).
- **Ubuntu 24.04 and later:** `sudo apt install ./SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Desktop-v1.27.0-amd64.deb`
- **Fedora 40 and later:** `sudo dnf install ./SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Desktop-v1.27.0-x86_64.rpm`

Server image: `ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalmusic:1.27.0` (the web player's themes ride
along with it).
