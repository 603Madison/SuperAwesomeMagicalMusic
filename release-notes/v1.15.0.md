## Search like a streaming app

Search now works the way Spotify's or Apple Music's does, in the web player and both apps. Every
source is asked at once and you get one set of results. The source each song comes from is never
shown.

- **Top result:** the likeliest thing you meant. Type an artist and you get the artist; type an
  album title and you get the album; type a song (or "song artist") and you get the song.
- **Songs, Artists and Albums**, each shown once even when several sources have it. Songs play from
  your library whenever it has them, as before.
- **Artist pages:** their best-known songs, their albums, and their singles and EPs, gathered from
  all your sources (an artist found on Qobuz still shows the albums in your library).
- **Album pages:** the songs in order, from your library when it has the whole album. Play,
  shuffle, download or add it all to a playlist.
- **Go to artist / Go to album** in every song's ⋮ menu.
- With only a Jellyfin library, an artist's name now finds their songs and albums, and an album's
  title finds its songs (Jellyfin itself only matches song titles).
- Android Auto: "play *artist* on SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic" plays their best-known songs, and
  "play *album*…" plays the album.

## Import playlists from more places

Import a playlist or album from a link to:

- **Spotify** (now albums too)
- **Apple Music**
- **Tidal**
- **Deezer**
- **ListenBrainz**
- **Qobuz** (read with your own Qobuz account)
- **YouTube / YouTube Music**

You can also import **from a list**: type or paste songs one a line ("Artist - Title"), or paste a
CSV export from Exportify, TuneMyMusic or Soundiiz.

Each song is found in your library first, then on your streaming services. Deezer's ISRC codes are
used to match exact recordings. Afterwards the app lists any songs it couldn't find.

Apple Music, Spotify and Tidal only publish the start of a long playlist on their public pages. When
that happens, the import says so.

In the web player and the server app, Import is on the Playlists page. In Lite, it's the link button
on the Playlists tab.

## Server

`/api/search` now also returns `top`, `songs`, `artists` and `albums`; the per-source `groups` are
still there for older apps. New endpoints: `/api/artist` and `/api/album`.
`/api/playlists/import` takes any of the links above, or `{"text": …, "name": …}`.
