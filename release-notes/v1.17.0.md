## Your devices work together

Like Spotify Connect: every web player and phone signed in to your account knows what the others
are playing, can control them, and can move the music between them.

- **See what's playing elsewhere.** When the web player is playing, your phone's mini player shows
  the song with **Playing on Chrome on Linux** in purple, with play/pause and skip. It works the
  other way too: the web player's bar turns purple and controls your phone.
- **Control it from here.** Tap the phone's mini player (or the devices button) for the other
  device's song: seek, skip, pause, and its volume. A phone's volume is its media volume, the one
  its own buttons change.
- **Move the music.** Choose **This phone**, or any device in the list, and the song carries on
  there, in the same queue, from the same moment. The device it left stops.
- **One plays at a time.** Start something on one device and the one that was playing pauses.
- On the web, the devices button sits in the player bar; on the phone, in the mini player and on
  Now Playing.
- The phone shows up while the app is open or playing, under its own name (Settings > About phone).

## Now Playing (web player)
