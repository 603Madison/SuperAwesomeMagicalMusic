## Importing playlists from a file

- **Choose a CSV or text file** instead of pasting it: in the web player, the
  SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic app and the Lite app (Import › From a list › Choose a file). The file's
  name names the playlist.

## Songs that aren't found stay in the playlist

- **A song an imported playlist names that isn't in any of your sources goes in anyway, greyed
  out**, in its place, marked "Not found". It's skipped when the playlist plays (and isn't
  downloaded).
- **It's looked for again by itself**: every few hours on the server, every 12 hours in the Lite
  app. When it turns up (Lidarr got the album into Jellyfin, say), the real song takes its place.
- **"Look for them again"** on the playlist looks right away.

## The web player's sidebar

- **Your radio**: your top four channels and stations (SiriusXM, Pandora or local radio), your
  favourites played most recently first, then others you've played lately. One click to play.
- **Your playlists** are listed by when you last played them, on any device (the app and Android
  Auto count too).

Server image: `ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalmusic:1.19.0`. Its database gains two
things (when each playlist was played, and which stations you play); it updates itself.
