## Fixes

- **SiriusXM Xtra channels keep playing past the first track** (e.g. Alt Nation Advanced
  Placement). SiriusXM hands out Xtra channels a track at a time, and each track's stream ends.
  The app now stitches them into one continuous stream: shortly before a track ends it asks
  SiriusXM for the next one and appends it, so playback carries on without stopping. The player
  also tells SiriusXM which track you're hearing, as its own apps do.
- If an Xtra stream breaks, the channel restarts at the current track instead of stopping.

If an Xtra channel still stops, play it until it does, then use Settings › SiriusXM ›
**Copy diagnostics** and send the result: it now records each step of fetching the next track.
