- Nextcloud sign-in now happens in the browser, so single sign-on through authentik works. The separate authentik sign-in is gone; playlists belong to your Nextcloud account. Playlists from 1.1.0 move over automatically. Sign in to Nextcloud again in Settings.
- Keep downloaded: in a playlist's menu, keep a whole playlist offline. Songs you add later are downloaded too, and copies are upgraded when a clearly better one turns up on any service.
- Better quality: off satellite, every service is compared and the best copy that fits your bitrate cap plays. Moving from satellite to Wi-Fi or cellular upgrades the song that's playing, and replays pick the right quality for the network you're on.
- SiriusXM Xtra channels continue to the next track instead of stopping after the intro. Settings › SiriusXM › Copy diagnostics helps troubleshoot if a channel still misbehaves.
- Bookmark SiriusXM channels with the star; they're listed first, in the app and in Android Auto.
- Fixed "NetworkOnMainThreadException" when testing Lidarr (and when signing in to other services). When Lidarr can't be reached from your current network, the settings are saved and requests wait.
