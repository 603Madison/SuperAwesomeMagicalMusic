## What's new

- **Song info**: "Info" in any song's menu (and the ⓘ button in the player) shows where the song
  was saved from and, while it plays, what's actually playing: the service, format, sample rate,
  bitrate, the bitrate cap that applied, and how much is buffered. Downloaded songs also show where
  the file came from, its bitrate and size.
- **Refresh downloads** (⟳ on a playlist): looks for songs from other services that Jellyfin now
  has, and re-checks every downloaded song straight away instead of waiting for the weekly check.
  Downloads from YouTube Music or Qobuz switch to the Jellyfin copy unless theirs is clearly better.
  In a kept playlist, missing or failed downloads are queued again.
- **Adjustable buffer** (Settings › Buffering): load from 30 seconds to 30 minutes ahead. Once a
  song has fully loaded, the next songs in the queue load too, so songs change seamlessly even
  when a satellite link drops out. The buffer uses up to 64 MB of memory.
- **Buffered range on the progress bar**: the player's seek bar and the mini player show how far
  ahead the song has loaded.
