## Fixes

- **Album art for songs saved from SiriusXM.** SiriusXM doesn't always send artwork (Xtra
  channels in particular), so these songs showed a blank cover. Missing artwork and album names
  are now filled in from another service's copy of the song:
  - when you save a song from SiriusXM,
  - when a song plays or downloads (from the copy that's playing),
  - for songs already saved: play them, or tap ⟳ (Refresh downloads) on the playlist to fill
    them all in at once.

  Artwork a song already has is never replaced.
