## YouTube Music plays again

- **YouTube Music songs stalled at 0:00** (the app showed the copy it picked, but nothing loaded).
  YouTube changed things in early November: the Android and iOS clients this app used to fetch
  songs stopped working, and the way download speeds are unlocked changed. The YouTube library
  (NewPipeExtractor) is updated to v0.27.0, which keeps up with both. This fixes the server (web
  player and the SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic app) and the Lite app.

Server image: `ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalmusic:1.19.1`.
