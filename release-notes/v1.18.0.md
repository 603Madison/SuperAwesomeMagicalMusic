## Yamaha MusicCast speakers

- **At home, your MusicCast speakers are among your devices.** Choose one in the devices list (in
  the web player's bar, or on the phone) and the music moves there, from the same moment. Pause,
  skip, seek and change its volume from the web player or the phone, like any other device.
- **How it works:** the server is somewhere else and can't reach your home network, so the phone
  does it. On your Wi-Fi it finds the speakers, tells them what to play and hands them each song
  (speakers only play plain addresses on the home network). While a speaker plays, the phone shows
  a notification and stays awake; it needs to stay at home on the Wi-Fi.
- **Settings › Speakers** lists the ones found, can add one by its IP address (when they can't be
  found on their own), and turns the whole thing off. If a speaker refuses something, why shows
  there (and as a message), so it can be fixed.
- Songs, radio stations and Pandora stations play on speakers; live SiriusXM channels can't yet.
- Someone playing something else on a speaker (its own app, AirPlay, the TV) takes it over; it
  stops being yours until you choose it again.

## Pandora stations as favourites

- **A heart on each station in the Pandora tab** (web player and phone), and on Now Playing while a
  station plays. Favourite stations sit with your channels at the top of Radio, one tap to start;
  in Android Auto too (Radio › Favourites).

## Details of what's playing

- **The (i) button on Now Playing** (web player and phone app) shows where the song comes from and
  what exactly is playing: the copy your server picked, its codec, sample rate and bitrate, your
  quality cap, volume levelling and how much is loaded ahead. Like the Lite app's.

## Now Playing, balanced

- The song's title and its buttons share one row, as wide as the cover; the channel above and the
  song's progress below line up with it too. The favourite heart is the same rose everywhere.
