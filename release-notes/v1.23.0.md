## The app, in the same look as your other apps

The SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic app had fallen behind the web player. It now shares the design of
SuperAwesomeMagicalWeather and Cockpit Manager, with the web player's violet and rose as its own
colours:

- **The app's own colours by default**: violet with rose for favourites, over the same cool slate
  neutrals as the other apps (navy in the dark theme), with a soft wash of violet at the top of
  every screen. It used the phone's wallpaper colours before; that's now a choice.
- **Settings › Appearance**: Light, Dark or System, and **Wallpaper colours** (Android 12 and
  later). The status bar follows the app's theme.
- **Every tab has a large title** (Search, Radio, Playlists, Downloads, Settings), and pages
  opened from them (artists, albums, playlists) have a back button.
- **Lists sit in rounded cards** with small headings above them, as in the other apps: search
  results, popular songs, albums' songs, playlists, downloads, channels by genre, stations, the
  queue and lyrics.
- **Search's top result is a hero card** in the violet-to-rose gradient; searches and filters use a
  rounded search field with a clear button.
- **Radio** switches between SiriusXM, Pandora and local radio with a segmented control.
- **Playlists**: new and import are at the top (no floating button over the songs); a playlist's
  rename, export and delete are in its ⋮ menu; "Offline" shows as a small label.
- **The mini player floats** as a rounded card above the tabs, with its progress along the bottom;
  when another device is playing it's edged in violet.
- **Now Playing**: rounder, raised artwork, a label for what's playing (Live, Downloaded or the
  copy), and a segmented Up next / Lyrics switch with the queue and lyrics in cards.
- **Empty pages** have a round icon, a title and a hint (and, for playlists, buttons to start).

(The SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic app only; the server, the web player and the Lite app are unchanged.)
