## YouTube Music on the phone

- **The phone now gets YouTube Music songs itself**, over its own connection, instead of through
  your server. YouTube turns servers away more and more (a colo server's address looks nothing
  like a listener's), and songs sat at 0:00 with nothing loaded. If YouTube doesn't answer the
  phone, or a song it fetched fails or doesn't start within 20 seconds, it comes through your
  server instead. Settings › Quality › **Get YouTube Music on this phone** turns it off.
- **The (i) details say who fetched the song**: this phone, straight from YouTube, or your server.
- **YouTube's audio is asked for the way NewPipe's own player asks** (the library this app uses
  for YouTube), on the phone, in the Lite app and on the server.

## Data Saver

- **The quality was capped at 128 kbps with Data Saver off.** Android also reports an app's data
  as restricted when that app's own "Background data" setting is off, and the app took that for
  Data Saver. "With Data Saver on" in Settings › Quality is now Original unless you choose a cap
  for it, and Data Saver is checked afresh each time a song starts.

## SiriusXM Xtra channels on the web

- **Songs on Xtra channels stopped partway, with a spinning wheel.** SiriusXM sometimes sends only
  the first part of a song; the rest was never fetched. The server now fetches it as it arrives.

## Finding out what's wrong

- **The web player's details (i) have a "Test this song's stream from your server" link**: it
  shows what your server sees when it fetches the song (found or not, how the service answered,
  how fast the audio came). For SiriusXM channels the link opens SiriusXM's stream log on your
  server.
- **The server logs a warning** when a service refuses a song or is slow to start sending it
  (`podman logs`).
