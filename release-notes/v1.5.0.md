## New

- **Data Saver bitrate** (Settings › Streaming quality): while Android's Data Saver is on, music
  streams at the Data Saver bitrate (96 kbps by default) or the network's own setting, whichever
  is lower. This applies even if the app is allowed unrestricted data in Android's Data Saver
  settings. Like Data Saver itself, it covers metered connections (cellular, satellite, metered
  hotspots), not ordinary Wi-Fi. Switching Data Saver on lowers the song playing too, and the
  player shows "Data Saver" next to the network while it applies.
