- SiriusXM: listen to live channels in the new Radio tab, and save the song you're hearing to a playlist. The library copy is used if Jellyfin has it; otherwise the artist is requested from Lidarr and the song plays from Qobuz or YouTube Music.
- Android Auto: browse playlists, downloads and SiriusXM channels, search, use voice requests, and save songs with the heart button.
- Lidarr requests wait in a queue until your Lidarr server is reachable (e.g. when you get home).
- Sign in with authentik: playlists belong to your account and sync to your own Nextcloud folder.

Installs over 1.0.0 and keeps your settings and downloads.
