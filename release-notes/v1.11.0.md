## SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic Lite

This app is now **SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic Lite**: the standalone app that talks to every service
itself, with no server needed. It updates over the installed app as before, keeping your settings,
playlists and downloads, and works exactly as it did.

The name makes room for what's coming: a SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic **server** for your homelab
(Docker/Podman, with accounts and Authentik sign-in), a **web player**, and an Android app that
signs in to the server. Lite keeps getting updates alongside them.

## Under the hood

- The music services (Jellyfin, YouTube Music, Qobuz, SiriusXM, radio, Lidarr, lyrics,
  scrobbling) moved into a shared module the server will use too.
- The release file is now `SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Lite-vX.Y.Z.apk`.
