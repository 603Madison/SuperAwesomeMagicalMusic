## SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic for Linux

A native desktop app for Ubuntu and Fedora: Rust with GTK 4 and libadwaita (GNOME's own toolkit),
playing through GStreamer. No web view, no Chromium; about 5 MB, and it starts at once.

- **Ubuntu 24.04 and later** (Mint 22, Pop!_OS 24.04, Debian 13):
  `sudo apt install ./SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Desktop-v1.26.0-amd64.deb`
- **Fedora 40 and later:** `sudo dnf install ./SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic-Desktop-v1.26.0-x86_64.rpm`

Open **SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic**, enter your server's address and sign in with your password or
your server's single sign-on (it opens the browser, which hands the sign-in back to the app).

- **Search** your library and every service, with artist and album pages.
- **Playlists** and **History** from every device.
- **Radio**: favourites, every SiriusXM channel with what's on, and local and internet radio; star
  channels and stations to keep them everywhere.
- **The player** with the queue, and **media keys and the desktop's media controls** (GNOME, KDE,
  the lock screen) with the song and its cover.
- What you play counts toward your **history and Wrapped**, live radio included (it shows as
  "Desktop app" among Wrapped's devices).
- **Preferences**: quality, and comparing every service for the best copy.

Checked on Ubuntu 24.04 and Fedora 42: the packages install with apt and dnf, GStreamer plays FLAC
and AAC over HLS with the plug-ins they bring, and the app starts. (Ubuntu 22.04's libadwaita is
too old for it.)

Server image: `ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalmusic:1.26.0` (unchanged apart from the
version).
