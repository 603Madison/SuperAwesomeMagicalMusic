## SiriusXM Xtra channels: finding the stall

Alt Nation Advanced Placement's log showed SiriusXM handing out plenty of songs, and the web player
stopping within a minute of the second song (which came from a different SiriusXM server than the
first). Nothing recorded why, so now:

- **The web player reports what it runs into on SiriusXM channels**: stream errors (with the
  segment, the SiriusXM server it came from and the HTTP status), stalls longer than 8 seconds,
  pauses it didn't get from the pause button, and each change of song in the stream. They go in
  SiriusXM's timeline, beside what SiriusXM sent.
- **A media error no longer stops the channel.** The player rebuilds its audio pipeline and
  carries on (the second time in a minute, also trying the other audio codec, as hls.js advises);
  only a third gives up.
- **The server notes what it couldn't relay** (a segment, playlist or key, and from which
  SiriusXM server), and once a minute how often the player reloaded the playlist, what's being
  heard and how much is queued.

## Claude connector

- **An admin's link can see every account.** Making a link as an admin, **See every account** (on
  by default) lets it see everyone's sources, SiriusXM, devices and listening history; the tools
  take an `account` to look at someone's search or history. Off: only the admin's own.
- **A server log tool** (`server_log`, admins): everything the server logged lately, not just
  warnings: each request with its status and time, relayed SiriusXM segments and keys,
  SiriusXM's timeline, what the connector was asked.
