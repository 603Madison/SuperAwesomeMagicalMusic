## New

- **Import playlists from a link** (link button on the Playlists tab). YouTube and YouTube Music
  playlists come in as they are; Spotify playlists are matched song by song to your Jellyfin
  library first, then YouTube Music, and you're told which songs couldn't be found. Spotify
  playlists are read from their public embed page, which lists up to about 100 songs.
- **Equalizer** (Settings › Playback): your phone's own presets (Rock, Jazz, Bass boost…) and a
  separate bass boost, applied to everything the app plays. Equalizer apps such as Wavelet can
  now work on the app's sound too.
- **Home-screen widget**: the song playing with its artwork, and previous, play/pause and next.
  Tap it to open the app. Play picks up where you left off even when the app isn't running.
