## SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic

The app is now called **SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic** (on your home screen, in Android Auto and in
Jellyfin's dashboard). It updates over the installed app as before, keeping your settings,
playlists and downloads.

## New

- **Even volume across services.** Songs from Jellyfin, YouTube Music, Qobuz and SiriusXM now
  play at about the same loudness, so you're not reaching for the volume knob between them.
  Jellyfin's loudness value (Jellyfin 10.9+) is used where a song has one, which keeps its
  dynamics; anything else is levelled gently while it plays, and boosts go through a limiter so
  they never clip. Most music ends up a little quieter than before, so you may want the volume a
  notch higher. Turn it off in Settings › Playback; a song's Info shows the gain in use.
- **SiriusXM songs from the beginning.** Tuning to a channel starts the song that's on from its
  start (as SiriusXM's app does) instead of partway through. Settings › Playback turns it off.
- **Restart song, back 15 seconds and Live** on live SiriusXM channels, in the player and as
  Android Auto buttons. Live appears once you're more than a minute behind.
- **Lyrics** (lyrics button in the player): from Jellyfin when the song is in your library and
  Jellyfin has them, otherwise from LRCLIB, a free lyrics database. Synced lyrics follow along,
  including for the song on a SiriusXM channel. Only the song's title, artist, album and length
  are sent, and only when you open lyrics.
- **Sleep timer** (moon button in the player): stop after 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes with a
  gentle fade, or at the end of the song. It works however playback started, Android Auto included.
