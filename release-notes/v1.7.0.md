## New

- **Thumbs up and down on Pandora stations**, in the player and in Android Auto, as in SiriusXM's
  app. They tell the station what you like; thumbs down also skips the song. Press a thumb again
  to take it back.
- **Bookmark songs in Android Auto.** The heart button is now a **+** that keeps the song you're
  hearing for later (it turns into a tick; press again to undo). The next time you open the app on
  your phone, it asks which playlist each bookmarked song goes in, or you can discard it or leave
  it for later. Songs heard on SiriusXM or a Pandora station are saved as usual: the Jellyfin copy
  if your library has it, otherwise the artist is requested from Lidarr.

## Changed

- **Codec info for Pandora stations.** Their tracks showed as "AAC-LC, 22.1 kHz". That's what
  the files declare, but files like these are usually HE-AAC at 44.1 kHz: they declare only their
  AAC-LC core, and the decoder adds the rest (spectral band replication). When the decoder does
  that, Info now shows "HE-AAC · mp4a.40.2 · SBR found by the decoder" and the real sample rate. At 64 kbps, HE-AAC sounds roughly like AAC-LC at twice the bitrate.
  As far as SiriusXM's web player shows, station tracks come in this one version only (its
  quality setting applies to channels, not stations), so there's no higher bitrate to ask for.
