## The desktop app catches up with the web player

The Linux desktop app now has what the web player has:

- **Now playing** as its own page: the cover large and the page washed in its colours, the
  **lyrics** following the song (click a line to play from there), **Up next** (play from, move,
  remove, clear), and on live radio the song on the channel and what it played before. Full screen
  from its corner.
- **Your devices:** the desktop app is one of them now, like the web player and the phone app. The
  devices button lists your web players, phones and speakers (Sonos, Music Assistant) with what's
  on each: play or pause them, or move the music there or here. While another device plays, the
  player bar controls it, and **Play here** brings it back.
- **Playlists:** import (a link, a typed or pasted list, a CSV file, or a playlist on your Subsonic
  server), export as CSV, and **share** one with people on your server, or leave one shared with
  you. Each song says who added it.
- **Radio:** your own stations, favourites, every SiriusXM channel, Pandora artist stations, and
  local radio (popular in your country, near you, or by name, your FM tuner's stations included).
- **History** and **Wrapped**, its story included.
- **Song details** in every song's ⋯ menu and on Now playing: where it's from, the copy your server
  picked, what GStreamer decodes and the volume levelling applied.
- **Even out volume** (Preferences): your library's loudness values where it has them, else gentle
  levelling as it plays.
- **Preferences** as the web player's Settings: appearance, quality, the DJ's voice, your sources,
  your name and password, where you're signed in, and signing in a flip phone with a code.
- **Admin** for admins: people, shared sources and the server's settings.

## Phone app

- **No more purple frame round the cover** on Now Playing. The cover was drawn at a fixed size
  inside a box that stretched to the screen's width, so the box's colour showed round it. It fills
  the box now, on every screen.
