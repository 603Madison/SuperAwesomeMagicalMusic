## Live radio on the server

The web player has a **Radio** page:

- **SiriusXM.** Add your SiriusXM streaming account in Settings › Sources (each person uses their
  own). Every channel in your plan, by category, with a filter; what's on now (song, artist and
  artwork), Xtra channels, and songs you hear can be saved to a playlist, where they play from
  your library or a streaming service.
- **Pandora artist stations** through SiriusXM: search an artist, start their station, and give
  thumbs up or down (down skips the song). More tracks are fetched as the station plays.
- **Local radio**: tens of thousands of stations from Radio Browser, popular in your country, by
  name, or near you. Song titles stations send are shown as they play. Stations that only stream
  over plain http play too (through the server), even when the web player is on https.

Everything streams through the server, including SiriusXM's encrypted streams (the server holds
the keys for your session), so it works wherever you can reach your server.

## Also

- A page of radio stations no longer holds up the music: the server gives up quickly on station
  logos that don't load (the browser only opens a few connections at a time).
- Songs saved from a Pandora station are kept as the song itself, since station links expire.
