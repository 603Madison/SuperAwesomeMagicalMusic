## Sign in once on each device

SuperAwesomeMagicalOrchestrator is the family's new member: you sign in to it once on a phone or
computer, and the family's apps there sign in by themselves. Music now takes part.

- **Phone app:** when you open it signed out, it asks the orchestrator on the phone and signs in
  as you. The sign-in screen also has **Continue as (you)** above the usual ways.
  - The first time, the button shows your server's address under it and waits for a tap, so
    you can see where it signs in.
  - The first time, the orchestrator also asks whether Music may sign in as you.
- **Mini:** the sign-in screen has a **Continue as (you)** row. One press of OK, and no code to
  type.
- **Linux app:** it asks the orchestrator's agent on your desktop when it starts, and the sign-in
  page has **Continue as (you)**.
- **Signing out stays signed out.** The apps don't sign straight back in. Use the button, or
  sign in any other way, when you want back in.
- **If it doesn't work** (no orchestrator, nobody signed in there, no account for you on the
  server), you get the usual sign-in, with no error.
- **Standalone** has no server, so nothing changes there.

## Carry on elsewhere

While music plays in the phone app or the Linux app, the orchestrator is told the song and
artist, so your other devices can offer to carry on with it.

- It's told once per song, and again every ten minutes while the same thing keeps playing (a
  radio station, a long mix).
- Pausing keeps it on offer for a while. Stopping or clearing the queue takes it away.
- **Settings › Show what I'm playing on my other devices** turns it off (on by default; in the
  Linux app it's in Preferences). Mini doesn't share what's playing.
- Nothing is sent unless the orchestrator is on the device and you've signed in through it.

## For whoever runs the server

- **Admin › Server › Orchestrator address** (or `SAMM_ORCHESTRATOR_URL`) turns it on, with a
  **Test** button. Left blank, nothing changes.
- On the orchestrator, list this server as the app `music`, with the address people use.
- **Whose account:** with single sign-on through the same identity provider, the same account
  and rules as signing in through it here. Otherwise the account with the same username, if it
  isn't tied to an identity provider.
- The break-glass admin never signs in this way, and accounts that are turned off stay off.
- Refused tickets are logged with the reason and slowed down like wrong passwords.

Update the server first.
