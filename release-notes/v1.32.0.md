## Your own stations: one song at a time

Songs on your stations (Your Radio, Rewind, Discovery) no longer overlap. Each song fades out,
there's a moment's quiet, then the next one starts from its beginning, as on the radio. A skip
fades out quickly instead of cutting.

## Subsonic servers

A new source: Subsonic servers, such as Navidrome, Gonic, Airsonic, Ampache, LMS, Nextcloud Music
and Funkwhale.

- **Adding one:** go to **Settings › Sources** and give its address with a username and password,
  or an API key.
- **It works like your Jellyfin library:** it's searched first, songs play from it first, Listen
  learns its songs, and Wrapped counts them.
- **Your playlists:** bring them in under **Playlists › Import › From your library**.
- **Your password stays on your server.** Requests are signed with a salted token, except for
  servers that can't check tokens (such as LDAP accounts), which get the password itself. Streams
  and covers go through your server, so the apps never see it.
- **Update the phone app along with the server.** Older versions can't open search results or
  playlists that have Subsonic songs in them.

## Sonos and Music Assistant: places to play

Your speakers now show up with your devices in the web player and the phone app. Move the music
to one and it carries on from the same moment. Pause, skip, seek and change the volume from any
of your devices.

- **Sonos:** add **Sonos** under **Settings › Sources**. An admin can share it with everyone, or
  you can add it just for you.
  - Speakers are found on the network, or you can add one speaker's address.
  - Grouped speakers play together, and the Sonos app shows the song, artist and cover.
- **Music Assistant:** add it under **Settings › Sources** with its address and a token. Its
  players then become places to play too: Sonos, Chromecast, AirPlay, Squeezebox, DLNA and Home
  Assistant's media players.
- **What plays:** songs, radio stations and your own stations play on speakers. SiriusXM's live
  channels don't yet.
- **Security:** a speaker plays each song from an address made just for that song, signed and
  short-lived, so it never needs your sign-in.
- **In a container:**
  - To find Sonos speakers by itself, the server needs host networking. Otherwise add one
    speaker's address.
  - If speakers can't reach the server, set the server's address for speakers on the source, or
    `SAMM_SPEAKER_URL`.

None of this has been tried with a real Subsonic server, Sonos speaker or Music Assistant yet,
only with stand-ins in tests.

Update the server first.
