## New

- **Pandora artist stations on SiriusXM.** Tap **Artist station** on a song playing on SiriusXM to
  start a station for that artist, or **New station** on the Radio tab to search for one. Stations
  are saved on the Radio tab and in Android Auto. Artist search uses a SiriusXM request that isn't
  publicly documented, so it may not work yet; starting from a song you're hearing doesn't need it.
- **Local radio.** The Radio tab has a new **Radio stations** section: find stations near you
  (asks once for your approximate location), search by name, and star favourites. Stations come
  from Radio Browser, a free, open directory. Favourites and nearby stations are in Android Auto
  under **Radio**.
- **App log** (Settings › About): records what Android Auto asks the app for and how it answers,
  and playback errors. Copy it after trying Android Auto so the problem can be pinned down.

## Fixes

- Android Auto: browsing now gives up with an error after 15 seconds instead of spinning forever,
  and a test now browses the app exactly the way Android Auto's media browser does (it passes).
