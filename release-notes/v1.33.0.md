## Sonos in the phone app

At home, the app now finds your Sonos speakers the way it finds MusicCast ones. You can also add
one by its address in **Settings › Speakers**.

- **Where they show:** each room, or rooms grouped together ("Living Room + Kitchen"), appears
  with your devices.
- **What plays:** your queue, through the phone, plus radio and your own stations.
- **Controls:** play, pause, skip, seek and volume. Grouped rooms use the whole group's volume.
- **The Sonos app** shows what's playing, and the next song follows on by itself.
- **No duplicates:** a Sonos your server already plays on isn't shown twice.

Your server can also play on Sonos speakers itself (since 1.32.0).

## Finding sources

Subsonic servers, Sonos and Music Assistant have been on the server since 1.32.0. Admins add them
under **Admin › Shared sources › Add a shared source**, the same way as Jellyfin. That list is now
grouped:

- **Your music library:** Jellyfin and Subsonic.
- **Music services and radio**
- **Places to play:** Sonos and Music Assistant.
- **Helpers**

If you can't add sources yourself, Settings now says where the admins add them.

## A lighter web player

For old or slow devices (old iPads, e-readers, smart TVs, a Raspberry Pi), the server has a second,
much lighter web player at `/lite`, for example `https://music.example.com/lite`.

- **Light:** pages of a few kilobytes and one small script that runs on old browsers. It even
  works with JavaScript off.
- **What it does:** the main web player's everyday things, kept simple. Music keeps playing while
  you move between pages.
- **Where to find it:** it isn't linked from the web player. Bookmark it on the device that needs
  it.
