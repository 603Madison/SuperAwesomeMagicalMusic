## Fixes

- **1.3.0 crashed as soon as it opened.** The new buffer setting's loader didn't pass every call
  on to ExoPlayer's own, and the first one the player makes when it starts threw an error. All of
  them are passed on now, and new tests start the player and the playback service so this can't
  ship again.

Everything from 1.3.0 is included: song info, refresh downloads, the adjustable buffer that runs
into the next song, and the buffered range on the progress bar.
