## New

- **A fresh look.** New colors (with Material You colors still used on Android 12 and up),
  rounder shapes and bolder headings throughout.
  - The player takes its colors from the album art: the sheet, progress bar and play button are
    tinted to match, with bigger artwork and controls, and the playing song highlighted in the queue.
  - The mini player floats above the tabs as a tinted card with its own progress bar.
  - Playlists are cards with a cover made from their songs' album art.
  - Search has a rounded search bar and a friendlier empty screen.
  - Songs without artwork get a soft gradient instead of a grey box.
- **Codec and bitrate for SiriusXM (and everything else).** Info (ⓘ in the player) now shows the
  codec the player is actually decoding, such as "HE-AAC · mp4a.40.5" or "AAC-LC", the real
  sample rate and channels, and the bitrate. Streams that don't state a bitrate (SiriusXM
  channels, Xtra channels, artist stations, radio) get one measured from the audio downloaded
  over the last dozen segments; where the stream does state one, both are shown.
