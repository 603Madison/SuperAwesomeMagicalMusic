## The SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic server and web player

Your own music streaming service, in a container:

```sh
docker run -d -p 8080:8080 -v samm-data:/data ghcr.io/603madison/superawesomemagicalmusic:1.12.0
```

(Podman works the same way. A Compose file is in `deploy/compose.yaml`, and the full guide is in
`server/README.md`.)

- **Accounts.** A local *break-glass* admin is made at first start (its password is in the log
  once, or set it with `SAMM_ADMIN_PASSWORD`). Add local accounts, or turn on **single sign-on**
  with Authentik (or any OpenID Connect provider): one group may sign in, another group makes
  admins, and accounts are made the first time someone signs in. The break-glass admin can always
  use its password, so a broken identity provider can't lock you out.
- **Sources for everyone, or just you.** Admins share sources with the whole server (your Jellyfin,
  Qobuz, Lidarr); YouTube Music is shared from the start. Everyone can add their own (their own
  Jellyfin or Qobuz account) and switch shared ones off for themselves. Saved sign-ins are
  encrypted, and only Jellyfin's and Qobuz's sign-in tokens are kept, not passwords.
- **A web player for the computer.** Search every source at once; songs play from your library
  first, then the best copy on a streaming service within your quality setting, like the app.
  Queue, playlists (import from Spotify or YouTube), history, synced lyrics, a sleep timer, volume
  levelling, media keys, and it's comfortable on a phone browser too. Set in Lexend.
- **Converted only when needed.** ffmpeg on the server converts a song only when it's over your
  bitrate cap or in a format your browser can't play (Opus where the browser has it, else AAC).
  Seeking works even in converted streams.
- **Lidarr.** When a song isn't in anyone's library, its artist is added to Lidarr (your own or
  the shared one); Settings shows what was added.
- **Artwork through the server**, so your Jellyfin needn't be reachable from outside.

Images are published for amd64 and arm64 (a Raspberry Pi works).

Coming next: SiriusXM, Pandora stations and local radio on the server, and an Android app that
signs in to it and keeps working offline and over satellite.
