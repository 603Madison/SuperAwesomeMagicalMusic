<p>A music player for self-hosters. It plays from your <b>Jellyfin</b> library first and fills the gaps from <b>YouTube Music</b> or <b>Qobuz</b>; artists you're missing are added to <b>Lidarr</b>. It also plays <b>SiriusXM</b> (live channels, Xtra channels and Pandora artist stations) and local <b>radio</b>, and works in <b>Android Auto</b>.</p>

<p><b>Made for the road</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Separate bitrates for Wi-Fi, cellular and satellite (T-Satellite and other direct-to-cell services), and a cap for Android's Data Saver.</li>
<li>A dead-zone cache that fetches the next songs while the signal is good, and downloads for offline listening that upgrade themselves to better copies.</li>
<li>In Android Auto: bookmark a song with one tap and pick its playlist later on the phone, thumbs up and down on Pandora stations, restart or rewind SiriusXM songs.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Listening</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Even volume across services, lyrics (Jellyfin or LRCLIB), a sleep timer, an equalizer and a home-screen widget.</li>
<li>Playlists synced to your Nextcloud (sign in through the browser, so SSO works), and playlist import from YouTube and Spotify links.</li>
<li>Listening history, including songs heard on SiriusXM, and scrobbling to ListenBrainz and Last.fm.</li>
</ul>

<p>Every account is your own: the app talks directly to your servers and services. SiriusXM, YouTube Music and Qobuz are reached through their unofficial or private APIs, which can change without notice.</p>
