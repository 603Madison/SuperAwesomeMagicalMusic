<p>The app for the <b>SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic server</b>: your own music streaming service, run in your homelab with Docker or Podman. Enter your server's address, sign in (with Authentik or any single sign-on, or a password), and your music, radio and playlists are all there.</p>

<p><b>Everything your server has</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Search like a streaming app across your Jellyfin library and every service your server has (Qobuz, YouTube Music and more): a top result, songs, artists and albums, with artist and album pages. Songs play from your library when it has them, else the best copy that fits your quality setting.</li>
<li>Import playlists from Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, Deezer, Qobuz, YouTube and ListenBrainz, or from a typed list.</li>
<li>SiriusXM channels (with what's on now) and Pandora artist stations with thumbs, through your own SiriusXM account on the server.</li>
<li>Tens of thousands of local and internet radio stations.</li>
<li>Your playlists, shared with the web player, with lyrics, a sleep timer and volume levelling.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Made for bad connections</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Offline: playlists are kept on your phone; changes made offline go to the server when you're back.</li>
<li>Downloads: single songs, or whole playlists kept offline and topped up as songs are added (on Wi-Fi by default), resumed where they stopped.</li>
<li>Satellite (T-Satellite and other direct-to-cell services): separate quality settings for Wi-Fi, mobile data and satellite; on satellite the player loads ten minutes ahead and pictures are kept small.</li>
</ul>

<p>Works in Android Auto (playlists, downloads, radio and search) and with headsets and the system's media controls.</p>

<p>Needs a SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic server. For an app that works on its own with no server, see SuperAwesomeMagicalMusic Lite.</p>
